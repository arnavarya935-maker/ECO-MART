# Textures Attribution & Credits

All 3D textures used in EcoMart are bundled locally in `/public/textures/` to guarantee 100% offline functionality.

## 1. NASA Blue Marble & Earth PBR Textures
- **Folder**: `/public/textures/earth/`
- **Files**:
  - `earth_day.jpg`: NASA Visible Earth Blue Marble equirectangular projection map (Public Domain / NASA).
  - `earth_normal.jpg`: Topography surface elevation normal map (Public Domain / Three.js).
  - `earth_specular.jpg`: Ocean roughness / water specular reflection mask (Public Domain / Three.js).
  - `earth_clouds.png`: Atmospheric cloud coverage layer with alpha channel (Public Domain / NASA).
  - `earth_night.png`: Global nighttime city illumination / anthropogenic light emissions (Public Domain / NASA Earth Observatory).
- **License**: Public Domain (NASA / U.S. Government works are free of copyright restrictions).
